# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (first release)

- A native SwiftUI and Metal graph of your Apple Notes: islands of related notes, people and organisation profiles, note links, mentions, topics and similar notes.
- Island colours across the graph, cards and pages; Liquid Glass island pills when zoomed out.
- A Maps-style drawer with search, islands, people, organisations and topics, and a page for each.
- Topics from Apple Intelligence on-device, or from keywords, switchable at any time; a clear notice when Apple Intelligence isn't available.
- A built-in sample library, with a guided Full Disk Access setup that swaps in your notes when access is on.
- Privacy: no network code, read-only access through a temporary copy, locked notes skipped, File → Delete NoteMesh Data.
